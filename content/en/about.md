## Plants and natural wellbeing

Carnet Végétal explores aromatherapy, gemmotherapy, Bach flower essences and everyday care through accessible guides and features.

## Who publishes this journal?

Carnet Végétal is an independent editorial publication. The editorial team picks its topics based on reader interest, with no commercial consideration and no sponsored content.

## A clear approach

Articles distinguish traditional uses, available evidence and practical precautions. Sources and publication dates make their context explicit.
