## Sources and context

Each feature identifies the sources used and distinguishes a traditional practice from a scientifically established effect.

## A place for nuance

Precautions, limitations and uncertainties are included when relevant. The publication does not provide individual medical advice.

## Clear authorship

Articles are signed by the collective editorial team, dated and updated when necessary. A brand mention does not imply that a product has been independently tested.
